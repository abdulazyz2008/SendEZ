How it works
1 · STUDENTS

2 · CERTIFICATES
A design per category
Winners, participants and volunteers each get a fitting template. Pick from 14 designs, or use your own from Canva.

Personal congratulations
Each student gets a personal email from your Gmail with their own certificate attached.
4 · PROOF
Can’t be faked
Every PDF is digitally signed and has a QR code. Before sending, each attachment is checked against its recipient.

How anyone can check a certificate
1
Scan the QR
Opens a public page: “Verified, issued to X for Y”. A fake ID shows “Not verified”.
2
Upload the PDF
Its fingerprint must match the signed original. Editing even one letter fails.
3
Check the issuer key
Ed25519 signatures; the public key is published so anyone can verify independently.

# SendEZ

> Upload your student list once. Every student gets their own congratulatory email with the right
> certificate attached, and every certificate can be verified as genuine.

## Download

| Computer | Download | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Mac** (2020 or newer, macOS 11+) | [**SendEZ-Mac.zip**](https://github.com/abdulazyz2008/SendEZ/releases/latest/download/SendEZ-Mac.zip) | Unzip, drag **SendEZ** into **Applications**, open it |
| **Windows 10 / 11** | [**SendEZ-Windows.zip**](https://github.com/abdulazyz2008/SendEZ/releases/latest/download/SendEZ-Windows.zip) | Unzip, open the **SendEZ** folder, double-click **SendEZ.exe** |

These links always give the newest version.

**The first time only**, your computer may warn you because the app isn't from an app store:
- **Mac:** press **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, press **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run anyway**.

## What it does

- Reads any Excel, CSV or Google Sheets list and fixes common problems (email typos, title rows, duplicates).
- Picks the right certificate design for each category (winners, participants, volunteers…).
- Emails every student their own digitally signed certificate from your Gmail or Outlook.
- QR code on every certificate; anyone can check it's genuine.
- Mahindra University club branding, scheduled sending, automatic continue after Gmail's daily limit,
  feedback stars, club reports, Add to LinkedIn, and a built-in Help chat.

Need help? Open SendEZ and press **Help**.
